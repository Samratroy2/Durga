About the Puja

A tradition carried by many hands

The Roy Bari Durga Puja is a living tradition. Its continuity comes not only from rituals, but also from the craftsmen, family members, photographs, memories, newspaper records, and stories passed from one generation to the next.

01 · The Idol Makers

The Hands Behind the Goddess

The making of the Durga idol is itself part of the Puja tradition. From the first framework to the final decoration, each stage depends on the skill and experience of the craftsmen.

A Craft Passed Down

Generations of craftsmanship

The idol associated with the Roy Bari Puja has been shaped through a continuing relationship between the family and its craftsmen.

Bamboo, straw, clay, sculpting, painting and decoration each require a different stage of careful work.

The finished Goddess carries the visual character of the Roy Bari tradition while also reflecting the individual skill of the present maker.
